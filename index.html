About

I'm a UI/UX and visual designer with a strong blend of design, technology, and creative problem-solving.

I craft user-centered digital experiences, visual systems, and brand-driven content that balance usability, aesthetics, and business objectives.

My work spans keyword research and content discoverability as an SEO intern at Google in Hyderabad, brand-consistent digital and print assets for L&T Metro Rail, and social media and promotional creatives for Auburn University at Montgomery.

Alongside design, I'm completing a Master's in Computer Science at Auburn University Montgomery and building full-stack and mobile projects, from a MERN bakery management system to React Native games and REST APIs.
